## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the serene, refreshing hospitality of an eco-conscious mountain village. Grounded in a modern, welcoming, and nature-centric visual language, it bridges pastoral charm with clear, accessible mobile utility. The interface serves multi-generational travelers, family vacationers, and community groups seeking authentic rural retreats.

The aesthetic blends warm organic minimalism with tactile warmth:
- **Atmosphere**: Tranquil forest greens, gentle sunlight amber, earthy terracotta touches, and soft cream foundations that evoke fresh mountain air without feeling sterile.
- **Mobile-First Accessibility**: Ergonomic touch targets, generous hitboxes, and intuitive thumb-zone layouts designed for effortless on-the-go navigation during travel.
- **Clarity & Comfort**: Soft pill-shaped contours, generous typographic breathing room, and structured information grouping to minimize cognitive fatigue for family trip organizers.

## Colors

The color palette reflects the verdant foliage, rich volcanic soil, and golden morning glow of the village slopes:

- **Primary (`#23533E` - Forest Green)**: Anchors identity, high-priority actions, brand headers, and primary confirmation flows. Conveys environmental stewardship, stability, and peaceful nature.
- **Secondary (`#C86D51` - Warm Terracotta)**: Used for highlights, promotional callouts, cultural experience badges, and warm tactile accents reminiscent of rural brickwork and pottery.
- **Tertiary (`#E5A93C` - Soft Amber)**: Reserved for ratings, seasonal notices, pricing highlights, and celebratory cues such as package discounts.
- **Neutral (`#2C342E` - Deep Moss Charcoal)**: Provides deep, readable text with softer contrast than harsh black, maintaining optical softness across daylight environments.
- **Surfaces & Canvases**: Built upon warm, breathable off-white tones (`#FBFBF7` and `#F4F3EB`), replacing clinical stark whites with welcoming organic canvas layers.

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans is utilized uniformly across headlines, body copy, and UI labels. Its geometric yet softly sculpted curves align seamlessly with friendly hospitality while maintaining pristine legibility on low-brightness outdoor phone displays.

- **Headlines**: Set in bold and semi-bold weights with subtle tight tracking on larger scales to produce strong, inviting focal points.
- **Body**: Uses regular weight with generous line height (1.45–1.55x) to guarantee effortless skimming of itineraries, accommodation amenities, and eco-guidelines.
- **Labels & Microcopy**: Balanced weights (semi-bold) ensure pricing tiers, badges, and metadata tags stay crisp and distinct across all screen densities.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces a fluid, responsive 12-column grid system optimized for thumb-driven mobile ergonomics:

- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column structure with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and page margins. Touch-friendly components span full width or comfortable two-column card grids. Interactive zones adhere to a 48px minimum vertical footprint.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px)**: 8-column layout with 24px margins, allowing split card configurations (e.g., package cards side-by-side with preview imagery).
- **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12-column bounded layout maxing out at 1200px width with 40px outer margins to preserve tight visual grouping without edge-stretching.
- **Rhythm**: Internal card and component padding is strictly managed using the `space-*` scale to maintain unified visual cadence between content blocks.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through soft, ambient tonal layering rather than harsh dropshadows. Surfaces simulate natural diffused lighting under an open canopy:

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas)**: Warm off-white canvas (`#FBFBF7`) providing an earthy, non-glare reading plane.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks)**: Pure pale cream (`#FFFFFF` with a 1px border of `#EAE8DD`) grounded by an ultra-subtle tinted shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(35, 83, 62, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns, Overlays & Sticky Bars)**: Elevated contextual controls utilize a wider ambient spread: `0 10px 24px -4px rgba(35, 83, 62, 0.10)` to separate active tools cleanly from underlying content.
- **Level 3 (Modal Dialogs & Floating WhatsApp Bar)**: Prominent focal elevation featuring `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(35, 83, 62, 0.16)`, signaling direct accessibility and tactile clickability above the viewport.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a pill-shaped and gently organic contour model (Roundedness Level 3). Sharp corners are eliminated to evoke natural comfort, warmth, and safe exploration:

- **Buttons & Tags**: Fully rounded pill shapes (`9999px` / `rounded-full`) to invite direct fingertip taps.
- **Cards & Sheets**: Large friendly corners (`2rem` / `rounded-lg` to `3rem` / `rounded-xl`) soften photographic borders and village activity snapshots.
- **Input Fields & Interactive Selectors**: Generous `1rem` baseline curvatures harmonize input fields with primary button styles.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button**: Pill-shaped with a minimum height of 48px (52px on mobile for thumb comfort). Solid Forest Green (`#23533E`) background, crisp white typography, and subtle forward chevron icon. Hover/active states subtly shift tone to a deeper foliage hue (`#1B4131`).
- **Secondary Button**: Outlined in 1.5px Forest Green with a light cream tint on press.
- **Warm Accent Button**: Solid Warm Terracotta (`#C86D51`) reserved exclusively for instant bookings and urgent promotions.

### Chips & Badges
- **Price Badges**: High-contrast, compact pills placed on top of package imagery. Features a subtle translucent dark scrim or warm cream backdrop with bold price tags (e.g., `Rp 150rb/org`) and soft amber star ratings.
- **Category Chips**: Horizontal scrolling filters (e.g., "Family Friendly", "Trekking", "Edukasi Tani") styled in soft sage-tinted backgrounds (`#EAF0EB`) that transition to solid green when active.

### Cards (Destinations & Packages)
- Built on Level 1 elevation with soft `2rem` rounded corners.
- Images feature a 4:3 or 16:9 aspect ratio with gently rounded upper borders, integrated floating price pill at top-right, and immediate overview specs (duration, group minimum, rating) below the title.
- Card footers contain a clear full-width or flex button pairing ("Lihat Detail" and "Booking").

### Form Fields & Inputs
- Height of 48px with `1rem` border-radius. Framed by a gentle border (`#D5D2C4`) that transitions to a 2px Forest Green outline on focus. Labels sit neatly outside the field for unambiguous screen-reader and visual clarity.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- 22px diameter touch targets with Forest Green check fills and smooth inner micro-animations for selecting tour add-ons (e.g., "Sewa Tenda", "Pemandu Lokal").

### Floating WhatsApp Booking Bar
- Persistent mobile dock anchored to the bottom screen safe area.
- Elevated at Level 3 with a dedicated quick-access WhatsApp button pairing: left segment presents selected package summary or immediate agent status, right segment presents a high-prominence green WhatsApp trigger (`#25D366` accent or brand `#23533E`) for frictionless family bookings.
